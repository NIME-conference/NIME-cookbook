## Reviewer invitation email

    You are invited as a reviewer for the workshop track at the 26th International Conference on New Interfaces for Musical Expression.

    The International Conference on New Interfaces for Musical Expression gathers researchers and musicians from all over the world to share their knowledge and late-breaking work on new musical interface design. The year’s conference, NIME [YYYY], will be held on the 23rd to 26th June in {Conference.City} and has the theme [THEME].

    Should you choose to accept our invitation, you will be assigned to review 1-3 workshop proposals. Review requests will be sent out after the 9th March. The deadline for completed reviews is 1st April. For more information, please refer to the NIME [YYYY] website (https://nime[YYYY].org).

    We expect to assign 1-3 workshop submissions to each reviewer, workshop submissions are short and should be quite easy to review.

## Review assignment email

    Dear reviewer,

    Many thanks for accepting the invite to review NIME [YYYY] workshop submissions.

    Workshop submissions have now been allocated, please log in to the CMT to check them:
    https://cmt3.research.microsoft.com/NIME[YYYY]/

    Please complete your reviews before the end of [REVIEW DEADLINE]. If you have conflict of interest with the submission you’ve been assigned please let us know.

    Please note that following are the requirements for a NIME workshop proposal:
    - Motivation: Describe the rationale for the workshop, issues that will be addressed, and clear objectives with reference to NIME topics, themes, and previous publications or workshops.
    - Workshop Structure: Describe the planned activities or events taking place in the workshop.
    - Organisers: Include Workshop organiser(s) with short bio(s) up to 200 words including background relating to proposed topic, and affiliation and contact information. Include additional speakers name(s), affiliation(s), and contact information.
    - Description of technical and space requirements: Including equipment required or brought by organisers; information on whether in-person or hybrid attendance is supported; expected size of event; and specific venue features.
    - Ethics statement
    - References

    You might also find the NIME paper review guide useful: https://github.com/NIME-conference/NIME-reviewing-guide/blob/main/paper-reviewer.md

    If you have any question or need any assistance please contact us at [workshops@nimeYYYY.org]


    Kind regards,
    [CHAIRS NAMES]
    NIME [YYYY] Workshop Chairs
