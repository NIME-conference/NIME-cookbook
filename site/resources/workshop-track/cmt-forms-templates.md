# CMT Form templates (examples from NIME [YYYY])

Usually the CMT is "cloned" from year to year so these forms should already be populated for you. You can edit them in the "Settings" panel (see top left). Then in the left menu select "Forms".

Placeholders that need replacing:

- `[YYYY]` by conference year
- `[LINK TO REVIEW SIGN UP]`
- `[CR DEADLINE]` by camera-ready deadline

## Submission form

(You might want to add fields here to indicate tech specs, workshop length, number of participants. See the camera-ready form. Using the form fields allows later exporting the information on a spreadsheet and facilitates the selection process)

     1. - Author Keywords
          List 3-5 keywords that accurately describe the workshop, separated with commas.

     Type: Comment 2. \* Reviewing for NIME
     In general, we expect that NIME authors contribute to the review process.

     Please consider signing up to review for NIME: [LINK TO REVIEW SIGN UP]

     Once you have considered signing up, then you can check the agreement box below. :-)

     Type: Agreement

## Meta-review form

     1. - (Visible to Authors and Reviewers) After careful deliberation by our volunteer reviewers and the Workshop Chairs, your submission has been given the following status:
          Type: Options with value
          Reject
          Accept
          Conditional Accept
     2. - (Visible to Authors and Reviewers) Enter your meta-review here. This includes your opinion of the workshop submission, incorporates the opinions of the other reviewers, and gives justification for your recommendation.
          Type: Comment
     3. (Optional) Use this box if you are recommending this workshop a "Conditional Accept." Please state why this is the case, and what specifically what the authors must do before the camera ready deadline in the case of Conditional Accept. Workshops with a conditional accept will need a final confirmation before being accepted.
     (Visible to Authors and Reviewers)
     Type: Comment

## Camera-ready form

     1. - Instructions for camera-ready
          Please ensure that your camera-ready workshop proposal addresses the feedback given by the reviewers and meta-reviewer. Additionally please ensure that it includes all sections listed in the call for workshops (https://nime[YYYY].org/call/workshops/) and uses the right template (in https://github.com/NIME-conference/NIME-template/releases, nime-music-workshop-latex-template.zip [Latex] or nime-music-workshop-template.docx [Word]).

     Please ensure that the following action items are completed by [CR DEADLINE] [YYYY]:

     - Your camera-ready submission and image (1000px in width) has been uploaded to this form, and you have completed all the requested items below.
     - At least one author has registered for full attendance at NIME [YYYY], either in person or online. (https://nime[YYYY].org/registration/)
     - The remaining authors are registered either for full conference attendance (online or in person) or workshop day only (https://nime[YYYY].org/registration/)
     - [For conditional accept only] How you have addressed the list of changes that were requested by the meta-reviewers for full acceptance.

     We are aware most of the information requested in the fields below is included in the proposal, however it makes our task as Workshop Chairs easier if you kindly include the information here, as we can then export all information into a spreadsheet. Many thanks!

     Type: Agreement 2. \* Camera-ready submission checklist
     Please ensure that your camera-ready submission PDF…
     – addresses the feedback given by the reviewers and the meta-reviewer.
     – uses the correct template ([Latex] nime-music-workshop-latex-template.zip or [Word] nime-music-workshop-template.docx, available in https://github.com/NIME-conference/NIME-template/releases/tag/v[YYYY].02.22).
     – has the appropriate sections as explained in the https://nime[YYYY].org/call/workshops/ (edit the template sections as needed)
     – includes an ethics statement.
     – matches the author names and order as listed in CMT.

     Type: Agreement 3. \* Workshop image
     Please ensure you have uploaded an image (1000px in width) for the conference programme.

     Type: Agreement 4. [Conditional accept only] Details of changes and edits for conditional acceptance
     [Conditional accept only] Here, you should detail:

     - How you addressed each condition from your submission’s meta review,
     - Where the change can be found (the general page and paragraph are fine),
     - Any other changes you have made that you feel support the acceptance of your workshop

     Type: Comment 5. \* Abstract / short description
     Please provide a short description/abstract of your workshop. This will be added to the NIME online programme. If you have set up a website for this workshop, please link it here too. It is fine to reuse the abstract of your proposal.

     Type: Comment 6. \* Workshop length
     Please indicate your workshop length.

     Half-day slots are 4h, Full-day slots are 8h with a 1h lunch break.

     We will be allocating only half-day or full-day slots, you are welcome to use the full slot you've been allocated even if you initially proposed a shorter workshop.

     Type: Options with value
     Full day
     Half day 7. \* Participation format
     Please indicate your workshop format

     Type: Options
     In person
     Hybrid
     Online 8. \* Technical Requirements
     Please list the technical requirements for your workshop in plain text format here.

     Examples:

     - A room with free space in the middle so that 10 people can move around comfortably
     - A room with 5 round tables so that participants can sit and work in groups
     - Stereo audio system
     - HDMI input to projector
     - WiFi connection
     - 5x power outlet
     - A zoom meeting

     Type: Comment 9. \* How many participants does your workshop support?
     We have a default workshop participation cap of 20 people per workshop. Please let us know if you would like fewer or more places advertised.

     We will be managing the workshop signups by circulating a sign-up form at the beginning of June.
     If you are planning to manage workshop participation yourselves (e.g. with an open call for submissions), please let us know here.

     Type: Comment 10. \* Workshop Proposal Release Agreement
     You are aware and accept that your submission PDF and image will be published on https://nime[YYYY].org under a CC BY 4.0 license.

     Type: Agreement 11. \* Confirmation of registration
     Please confirm that at least one of the authors has registered to the full conference, and that the remaining authors have at least registered for workshop attendance (https://nime[YYYY].org/registration/)

     Type: Agreement
